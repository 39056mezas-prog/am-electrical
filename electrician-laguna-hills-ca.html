Laguna Hills is one of the more compact cities we serve — about 6.7 square miles, incorporated in 1991, built out mostly from the early 1960s onward across its hillside terrain. It sits right next to Laguna Woods Village, one of the largest age-restricted communities in the country, and a lot of our Laguna Hills calls come from homeowners who've been in the same house for decades and just want a panel, a circuit or a fixture handled properly, without a hard sell.
