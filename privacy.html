What we collect

This website collects only what you choose to submit through the contact form on the homepage: your name, phone number, email address, service type, property or project type, and any message you include. Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your request for electrical service — to follow up, schedule work, and provide a quote. We don't sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.
