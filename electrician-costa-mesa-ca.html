Older homes,
a real commercial core.

Costa Mesa incorporated in 1953, decades before most of the other cities we serve existed, and it shows: neighborhoods like Mesa Verde and College Park are full of original mid-century single-story homes from the 1950s and 60s, many still on close to their original electrical systems. At the same time, Costa Mesa is home to the South Coast Metro business district and the Westside's mix of light industrial and creative office space — real commercial demand, not just residential. We get called for both, often in the same week.
