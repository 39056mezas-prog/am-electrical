### Page hero

- Copy: "AM Electrical is a fully licensed California electrical contractor (CA Lic. #681767), carrying both a contractor's bond and insurance coverage. Here's what that actually means for you."

### Explainer (ledger)
- **Licensed** — "AM Electrical holds California Contractor's State License Board license CA Lic. #681767. A license means the contractor has met the state's requirements to perform electrical work legally. You can look up any California contractor's license status, classification and history directly on the CSLB website." (links to `cslb.ca.gov/681767`)
- **Bonded** — "California requires licensed contractors to carry a contractor's bond. A bond is a financial guarantee, backed by a surety company, that exists to protect customers if a licensed contractor fails to meet certain legal or contractual obligations. It isn't insurance for the contractor; it's a layer of protection for you."
- **Insured** — "AM Electrical carries insurance coverage for the work performed. Insurance is what stands behind a job if something unexpected happens during the course of work, separate from, and in addition to, the state-required contractor's bond."
- **Why it matters** — "Hiring a licensed, bonded and insured electrician means you're working with a contractor who has met California's requirements to do the work legally, and who carries real financial protection behind that work, not just a verbal promise."
