When it can't wait
until morning.

Most electrical problems can wait for a scheduled appointment. Some can't. If something feels actively unsafe — a smell, a sound, a spark — treat it like an emergency and call. We answer 24 hours a day, every day, and dispatch a licensed electrician, not an answering service reading from a script.

This counts as an emergency
01No power to part or all of the building
02Burning smell from an outlet or panel
03Sparking outlet or switch
04A breaker that won't reset
05Water anywhere near a panel or outlet

What we actually get called out for.

Not a hypothetical list, this is the range of calls that come in after hours: a main breaker that won't reset, a smell no one can place, a room that suddenly went dark. Someone answers, and a licensed electrician is on the way.

What to do
until we get there.

A few safe steps while you're waiting for us to arrive, if you're able to take them:

  • Cut the power. If you can safely reach it, switch off the breaker for the affected circuit, or the main breaker for anything serious.
  • Clear the area. Keep people and pets away, especially if water is involved anywhere near the issue.
  • Don't touch it. Exposed wiring, a hot outlet, or a panel that's buzzing or smells like it's overheating — leave it alone.
  • Call, don't guess. We'll walk you through anything else over the phone while we're on the way.
