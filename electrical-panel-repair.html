Signs your panel needs attention.

A panel rarely fails without warning first. These are the signs that get people to finally call.

Breaker trips repeatedly Still running on a fuse box Panel cover feels warm Flickering lights across the house Burning smell near the panel Buzzing or crackling sounds Visible rust or corrosion Adding an EV charger or hot tub

Repair, replacement or upgrade?

  • Repair. One breaker or connection has a specific fault. Fix it, and the rest of the panel is fine.
  • Replacement. The panel or fuse box itself is old, undersized or failing broadly enough that patching isn't the right call.
  • Upgrade. Nothing's broken — there's just not enough capacity left for an EV charger, a hot tub, an addition, or a home that's added more load over the years.
  • Code compliance. Bringing an older panel up to current code, often needed for a sale, refinance or permit.
